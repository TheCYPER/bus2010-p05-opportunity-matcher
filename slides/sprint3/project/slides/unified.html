Team 5, P05 Opportunity Matcher

5

The professor asked every team to make the app look unified. The class has one shared app that started from Team 1’s project, and it still had the generic look the tool generated. We gave the whole app one look through its shared design tokens: the same paper background, teal and lime as our prototype, our two typefaces, flat surfaces and a small set of status colours that always come with a word. Because it is done through tokens, every team’s page changes at once and no one’s module has to be rewritten; the app’s 73 tests still pass. It is pull request number 2 on the shared repository, and number 3 adds our module to its navigation.
