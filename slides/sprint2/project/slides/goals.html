User stories

Student

I want my top 3 internships with plain reasons, so I know where to apply first.

Student

I want to see the one missing skill and send it to training, so I can unlock a job.

Coordinator

I want to confirm or reject each AI skill suggestion, so no profile changes without a person.

Partner team

We want one shared database with our own write key, so all seven modules use the same records.

We are Team 5, and our part of the co-op platform is P05, opportunity matching. In Sprint 1 the matching was rules only, and the “AI helper” was simulated word matching. In Sprint 2 we made the AI real and connected the prototype to live data that all seven teams can share. Our goal: show each student which open internships fit and why, using AI that reads meaning, while the AI never decides who can apply. We set two success criteria and both are measured: the AI changed eligibility in zero of 36 student–job pairs, and every AI result is labelled with its model and method, with every skill suggestion waiting for a person.
