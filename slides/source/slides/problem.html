Our persona is Layla Hassan, a student who wants an aviation analytics internship in Dubai. Finding jobs is already another team’s module: Layla can browse listings. What she cannot do is answer three questions about a given job. Three of the four acceptance criteria in our brief are about explaining results honestly, not about ranking accuracy, so we treat the explanation as the product. One scope rule we fixed early: we rank jobs for students, never students for employers.
