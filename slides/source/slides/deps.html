This is our side of the shared dependency board. Every input has a backup we control, so no sprint goal waits on another team: profiles and job listings come from our own demo data for now, and the skills-training team is simulated. The one handoff that has to become real is with P06, because it is the only one where our output changes their work and their output changes ours. At the next Scrum of Scrums we will bring three asks: agree the fields with P06 and schedule a joint test, settle who owns the shared skill list, and agree that P01 saves a new profile version when a skill is verified, so that our module never stores its own judgments about skills. These asks are our proposals; the other teams have not agreed to them yet.
