Demo 3: help for newcomers and for staff

A guided tour, and a coordinator view

First-time visitors get a 6-step tour and a glossary. A “How it works” page explains the whole co-op journey.

Students can ask about a result. The coordinator sees exactly what it used and can leave a correction note.

Team 5, P05 Opportunity Matcher

8

Two kinds of help. Many students have never looked for a co-op placement, so the first visit opens a short welcome page that explains the seven-step journey, followed by a six-step tour of the screen; a glossary is always one click away. The second persona is Sara, the co-op coordinator. Arjun, another demo student, asked why Jira shows as missing when he runs an agile board every week. Sara can see exactly which inputs the result used, and nothing else from his profile. The cause: he typed a free-text phrase the system does not recognise as Jira. She leaves a correction note for the profile team; our module never edits another team’s records.
