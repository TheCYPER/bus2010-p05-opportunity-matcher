This is the decision everything else builds on. Each requirement has three possible answers, not two. Can apply needs verified evidence. Not a fit now is a real conflict, like a remote-only student and an on-site job. One thing to do means we simply don’t know yet: the data is missing or the skill was never checked. A system that treats “unknown” as “no” tells Layla she does not qualify when the truth is that nobody has checked. Our brief warns against exactly that kind of false confidence, so “one thing to do” is its own answer and the interface never hides it.
