3

Seven student teams each build one step of the co-op platform. A student builds a profile, employers post jobs, the university approves them, then our step: which of those jobs fit this student. After us come skills training, applications and the placement itself. We read profiles from P01 and approved jobs from P03. Our one two-way hand-off is with P06: when a skill is missing, we send it to skills training; when they verify it, P01 saves a new profile version and we refresh the match. We never store our own opinion of whether a skill is verified; that belongs to P06.
