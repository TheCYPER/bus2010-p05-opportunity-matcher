We use three channels, each with one job. WhatsApp for the daily written check-in: what I finished, what is next, where I am stuck. Teams for our twice-weekly calls, shared files and sprint reviews. Notion is the task board, and every task there has an owner and a reviewer. Once a week our representative joins the Scrum of Scrums with the teams we depend on. The ground rules at the bottom come from the team charter we all signed on 23 October: urgent messages answered within 24 hours, absences flagged four hours ahead, and a three-step way to settle disagreements.
