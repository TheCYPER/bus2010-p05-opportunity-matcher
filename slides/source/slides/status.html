We want to be clear about what is real. Works now means it runs and keeps its records in the browser. Simulated means we stand in for something we do not control yet: the partner teams’ student and job data, the skills-training team’s review step, the AI part, and sign-in. Planned means it has a sprint attached: the joint test with P06 and our first test with classmates in Sprint 2; the coordinator-labelled comparison set and free-text skill mapping in Sprint 4. The binder asks us to keep these three apart, and the prototype says the same thing on its Partner teams page.
